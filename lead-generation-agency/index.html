How we build it

A lead program in three parts

Every one of these has to work. A great ad sending people to a bad form produces the same result as a bad ad.

1. Capture the right demand

Google Search and Local Services Ads for people already looking for what you do. Meta Ads for people who will need you soon but haven't started searching. Budget goes where qualified leads cost least.

2. Convert with less friction, not none

Landing pages and forms that ask what your intake team needs, filter out people who aren't a fit, and make calling easy for the people who are.

3. Close the loop

Calls and forms tracked to the source, lead outcomes imported back into Google Ads and Meta, and reporting in cost per qualified lead and closed revenue.

Sound familiar?

The leads come in. The deals don't.

Most businesses that ask us about lead generation aren't short of leads. They're short of good ones. The agency reports a falling cost per lead, the sales team reports that half the numbers are dead, and both of them are telling the truth.

This happens because ad platforms optimize toward what they can see. If the only conversion they're told about is a form submission, they get better and better at finding people who submit forms. Whether those people answer the phone, qualify, or ever buy is invisible to them.

Lead quality is a tracking problem before it's an advertising problem. Once the platform knows which leads became customers, it can go and find more of them.

What our lead generation services include

  • Channel plan. Where your budget should go between Google Search, Local Services Ads and Meta Ads, based on how people look for your service and what a qualified lead is worth to you.
  • Landing pages and forms. Pages built for one offer and one action, with forms that ask the qualifying questions your team needs. Removing every question makes leads cheaper and worse; we test where the balance sits.
  • Call tracking. For most service businesses the phone is the main conversion. Calls are tracked to the campaign and keyword that produced them, and separated from wrong numbers and existing customers.
  • Offline conversion import. Every inquiry carries the Google click ID. When a lead qualifies or closes, that outcome goes back into Google Ads, and into Meta through the Conversions API, so bidding optimizes toward customers instead of contacts.
  • Reporting you can check against the bank account. Cost per qualified lead, cost per booked appointment, and closed revenue by channel, not impressions and clicks.

Speed to lead matters more than most settings

A lead contacted within minutes behaves very differently from one contacted the next day. By then they've usually called two competitors and forgotten your ad. We'll look at how leads are handled once they arrive, because no amount of campaign optimization makes up for a slow follow-up.

That might mean routing form notifications to the right phone, adding a booking option so people can schedule themselves, or simply agreeing on who calls back and how fast. It's not glamorous, but it's often the cheapest improvement available.

Who we generate leads for

  • Healthcare and behavioral health providers, including ABA therapy practices, where an inquiry has to survive insurance verification before it becomes a client. See Google Ads for ABA therapy.
  • Home services, such as HVAC, plumbing, roofing and remodeling, where the phone call is the lead and Local Services Ads usually belong in the mix.
  • Self-storage facilities, where the lead is a reservation and the number that matters is move-ins. See Google Ads for self-storage.
  • Professional services and B2B, where one client can be worth years of revenue and lead quality outweighs lead volume every time.

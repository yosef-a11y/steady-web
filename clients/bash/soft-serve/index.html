Questions

Good to know before you book.

Is it kosher?

Yes. Our soft serve is certified by the ORB, and cholov yisroel is available. We also have non-dairy for meat meals.

Where do you serve?

All of South Florida: Palm Beach, Broward and Miami-Dade counties. If you're just outside that, ask and we'll let you know.

How many machines do I need?

Each machine pours 60 to 75 servings an hour and serves one flavor. For most events, one machine per 70 or so guests keeps the line short. Want two flavors? That's two machines.

How long does the attendant stay?

Up to 5 hours at no extra charge with a full-service station.

Can it go outside?

In the cooler months, yes. In summer, South Florida humidity is too much for the machines, so summer setups are indoors.

What do you need from us?

An indoor spot near a standard outlet on its own circuit breaker. We bring extension cords. For a basic setup, we'll ask for a table and tablecloth.

Can we use our own logo or flavor?

Yes. We can print your logo on cups and napkins, brand the cart, and make a custom flavor. Mention it in the form and we'll include it in the quote.

What this is, in plain English

1. What the law wants

Fire code says emergency responders must be able to use their radios everywhere in your building. Your fire official checks this before signing off on the building.

2. Why buildings fail it

Thick concrete, steel, energy-efficient glass and underground levels all block radio signal. Most modern buildings can’t pass on their own.

3. How it gets fixed

An antenna on the roof picks up the signal, an amplifier boosts it, and antennas spread it through the building. The industry calls it a BDA or ERRC system.

No radio coverage, no certificate of occupancy.

It is one of the few things that can hold a finished building empty. We test before your inspection is booked, and fix whatever the test finds.

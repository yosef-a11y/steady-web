# Landing Page Psychology: 9 Principles

| # | Principle | Do | Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Affective priming | Hero image and headline set the right emotion before reading. Purposeful images that show the outcome. | Fear/burnout imagery, tone that shifts, decorative stock images |
| 2 | Selective attention | Assume people scan. Pattern-interrupt, then point attention at the CTA. Remove distractions. | Hooks with no next step, competing CTAs or links |
| 3 | Proof stacking | Proof after every major claim, all the way down the page. Repeat the core promise. Show volume. Native-looking proof (screenshots, DMs, raw video). | Carousel-only proof, generic praise, all proof at the bottom |
| 4 | Chunking | Subheads, lead bullets, named numbered frameworks, white space, one idea per block | Walls of text, ungrouped lists, crammed layout |
| 5 | Authenticity | Raw, real assets and founder voice | Staged/fake assets, sterile corporate tone |
| 6 | Narrative transportation | Open with the reader's "before" state; offer comes in as the bridge; show the transformation | Opening with the offer, feature dumps, generic case studies |
| 7 | Curiosity gap | Headlines/leads with tension and a specific payoff ("even if you...") | Flat big claims, clickbait the page never pays off |
| 8 | Loss aversion | Show the cost of waiting; real deadlines and scarcity; balance with gain | Fake urgency, fearmongering, loss-only framing |
| 9 | Authority bias | Credentials high on the page; borrow authority (client/media logos, peer testimonials, stage photos) | Invented credentials, buried authority, badge overload |

## Scoring
Score each principle 0 (absent/wrong), 1 (weak) or 2 (strong). Max 18. Any 0 = priority fix.

## Page order
1. Hero: priming, curiosity headline, CTA, authority above the fold
2. "Before" story
3. Offer chunked into a framework
4. Proof stacked after each claim
5. Real urgency next to the CTA
6. Final CTA with a proof recap

## Pre-ship checklist
- One emotional tone throughout
- Every attention element leads to the CTA
- Testimonials are specific (numbers, timeframe, outcome)
- Proof in 3+ places
- No block longer than ~4 lines; every section has a subhead
- Authority visible without scrolling
- All urgency, scarcity and credentials are true

## Guardrail
Never invent proof, urgency, scarcity or authority. If inputs are missing, ask for them.

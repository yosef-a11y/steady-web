Consultant or agency?

Why the answer is both, and why it matters

The consulting model has a structural flaw: the person with the insight is never the person with their hands on the account. A consultant delivers a growth strategy that assumes a level of execution the client's agency, or in-house team, then fails to deliver. Six months later the strategy is blamed, or the agency is, and nobody can tell which.

The agency model has the opposite flaw. Execution is fine, but nobody with real authority is asking whether the channel mix is right, whether the tracking is lying, or whether a 4x return on ad spend on a 30% margin product is actually losing money. Campaigns get optimized toward whatever metric is on the dashboard.

Steady Growth Marketing is built so those two jobs are done by one person. The strategist who audits your business and writes the plan is the one who logs into your accounts on Monday morning. When the plan is wrong, we find out in week two, not quarter two, and we change it.

What we measure

A growth marketing consultant is only as useful as the numbers they hold themselves to. These are ours, defined plainly:

  • Return on ad spend (ROAS): revenue generated per dollar of ad spend. Useful, but meaningless without margin.
  • Cost per acquisition (CPA): total ad cost divided by the number of customers or qualified leads it produced. The number most businesses should optimize toward.
  • Contribution margin after ad spend: what a sale leaves behind once product cost, shipping, returns, and the ad that produced it are paid for. The number that decides whether growth is worth having.
  • Payback period: how long a new customer takes to return the cost of acquiring them. What tells you how hard you can push.

Before any of these can be trusted, conversion tracking has to be right. Fixing tracking is where most engagements start, because optimizing toward a number that's wrong makes things worse faster.

How an engagement runs

  1. Free audit. You share access; we review account structure, wasted spend, tracking accuracy, and competitive position. You get the findings as a list of specific opportunities, whether or not you hire us.
  2. Plan. Within two weeks: budget allocation by channel, target CPA or ROAS, the first three tests, and a 90-day roadmap.
  3. Run. We execute inside accounts you own. Tracking is verified before the first dollar moves.
  4. Report and revise. Monthly reporting in revenue and cost per lead, a standing call, and a plan that changes when the data says it should.

Straight answers

Growth marketing consulting, questions answered

A consultant tells you what to do; an agency does it. The gap between the two is where most plans die, because the consultant's deck assumes an execution quality the agency's junior team never delivers. Steady Growth Marketing closes that gap by having the strategist run the account. The plan and the execution come from the same person.

Paid media is the core: Google Search, Shopping, Performance Max, Display, and Meta Ads.
